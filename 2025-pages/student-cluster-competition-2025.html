Background Information

With sponsorship from hardware and software vendor partners, competing student teams design and build small clusters, learn scientific applications, and apply optimization techniques for their chosen architectures in a non-stop, 48-hour challenge.
Learn more about this competition: https://sc25.supercomputing.org/students/student-cluster-competition/

Preparation

The team was formed in May 2025 and we submitted our application to the Student Cluster Competition 2025 (SCC25) committee. We started fundraising for hardware and for airplane tickets shortly after.
